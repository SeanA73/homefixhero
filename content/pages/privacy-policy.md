This Privacy Policy explains how HomeFixHero ("HomeFixHero," "we," "us," or
"our") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit
homefixhero.com (the "Site").

## Information we collect

- **Information you provide.** When you contact us or subscribe to
  updates, we collect the information you provide, such as your name and
  email address.
- **Automatically collected information.** Like most websites, we
  automatically collect certain information through cookies and similar
  technologies, including your IP address, browser type, device
  information, pages visited, and referring URLs.
- **Analytics.** We use analytics tools to understand how visitors use the
  Site so we can improve our content and user experience.

## How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

- Operate, maintain, and improve the Site
- Respond to your inquiries
- Send updates you've opted in to receive
- Monitor and analyze usage trends
- Detect, prevent, and address technical issues or abuse

## Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences, measure
Site performance, and, where applicable, serve relevant advertising. You
can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling cookies may
affect Site functionality.

## Advertising and affiliate links

Some pages on the Site contain affiliate links. If you click one and make
a purchase, HomeFixHero may earn a commission at no additional cost to
you. This does not influence our editorial recommendations.

## Third-party services

We may use third-party service providers (such as analytics, hosting, and
email delivery providers) that process data on our behalf, subject to
their own privacy practices.

## Data retention

We retain information only as long as necessary for the purposes described
in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

## Your choices

You may opt out of marketing emails at any time using the unsubscribe link
in any email, or by contacting us directly.

## Children's privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated"
date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
