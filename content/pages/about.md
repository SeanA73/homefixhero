HomeFixHero exists for one reason: home repairs are confusing, and bad
advice is expensive. We publish clear, practical guides — plumbing,
electrical, HVAC, and the tools behind them — written so you can actually
follow them without a trade background.

## What we cover

- **How-to guides** for the repairs homeowners run into most often
- **Cost guides** so you know a fair price before you call a contractor
- **Product reviews and comparisons** for the tools worth buying

## Our approach

Every guide is written to be practical first: what to check, what tools
you need, when a DIY fix is reasonable, and — just as important — when a
job is genuinely a call-a-professional situation. We'd rather tell you not
to attempt something than let you get hurt or make a problem worse.
