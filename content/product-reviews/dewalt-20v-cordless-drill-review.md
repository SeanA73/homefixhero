For most homeowners, the DEWALT DCD771C2 hits the sweet spot between price
and capability. It's the drill we reach for on everything from hanging
shelves to assembling furniture to driving deck screws.

## Performance

The two-speed transmission (0-450 and 0-1,500 RPM) covers the range a
homeowner actually needs — low speed for driving screws without stripping
them, high speed for drilling through wood and light metal. At 300 unit
watts out (UWO), it has enough torque for most household tasks, though it's
not intended for continuous heavy-duty framing work.

## Battery and runtime

The kit ships with two 1.3Ah batteries, which is enough to get through a
weekend project list without needing a mid-task charge. Serious DIYers who
plan to run it daily may want to upgrade to a higher-capacity 4.0Ah or
5.0Ah battery, both of which are compatible.

## Build quality

The compact 7.6-inch length makes it noticeably easier to use in tight
spaces — under sinks, inside cabinets, behind furniture — than older
full-size drills. The three-jaw chuck is solid, though we noticed slight
loosening after extended high-torque driving; a quick re-tighten every so
often solves it.

## Who it's for

This is the right drill for homeowners who want one reliable tool for
general repairs, furniture assembly, and small projects, without paying a
premium for brushless technology they won't use often enough to justify
the cost.

## Verdict

At its typical street price, the DEWALT DCD771C2 remains one of the best
value cordless drills for everyday home use in 2026.
